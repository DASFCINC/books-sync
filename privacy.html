Privacy policy

Books Sync · effective November 10, 2026

Books Sync is a private tool used by one company's owner. It is not available to anyone else. This policy explains what it does with QuickBooks Online data.

What it reads

With the owner's permission, it reads the company's accounting data from QuickBooks Online: the chart of accounts, financial reports, and transactions with their amounts, dates, accounts and vendor names. It never writes to QuickBooks.

What it does not keep

Customer names and the free-text memo, description and note fields are dropped before anything is saved. It does not collect employee records, tax identification numbers, bank account numbers or attachments.

Where the data goes

  • The owner's own computer, and a database project that only the owner controls.
  • When the owner asks the dashboard's assistant a question, totals and vendor names relevant to that question are sent to the AI service that answers it, and the answer can be sent to the owner's own private messaging chat. Customer names and memo text are never sent.

The data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than the owner's own bookkeeping, planning and tax preparation.

Security

Access tokens are encrypted on the owner's computer. Connection keys are kept in a secrets manager, not in files or code.

Disconnecting and deletion

The owner can disconnect at any time from QuickBooks Online (Apps, then Disconnect). After that the tool can no longer read the books. Stored copies can be deleted by the owner at any time.
