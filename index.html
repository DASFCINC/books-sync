Books Sync

A private tool for one company. It is not offered to the public.

Books Sync reads one company's QuickBooks Online books, read-only, into that company owner's own finance dashboard. Only the owner uses it, from the owner's own computer.

Connecting

There is nothing to sign up for here. The owner connects QuickBooks from their own computer with the tool's connect command. Other QuickBooks users cannot connect to it.
